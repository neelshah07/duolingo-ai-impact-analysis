# 🦉 Duolingo AI Strategy Impact Analysis

## Review Intelligence, Hidden Dissatisfaction & Business Impact

An end-to-end Data Science and Business Analytics project that analyzes **Duolingo Google Play reviews** using AI-based sentiment analysis to uncover customer dissatisfaction that traditional star ratings can miss.

The project combines **data collection, NLP, sentiment analysis, business pain-point detection, hidden dissatisfaction analysis, strategic prioritization, and an interactive Streamlit dashboard**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

Traditional app ratings do not always reveal what users actually feel.

A user may give an app **4 or 5 stars** while still complaining about:

- Energy systems
- Advertisements
- Subscription pricing
- App features
- Monetization
- Bugs and crashes

This project investigates this gap between:

> ⭐ **What users rate**  
> vs.  
> 🤖 **What users actually say**

The objective is to identify hidden dissatisfaction and translate customer feedback into actionable business strategies.

---

# 📊 Key Findings

Based on the analyzed dataset of **10,000 Duolingo Google Play reviews**:

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Reviews Analyzed | **10,000** |
| AI Positive Reviews | **8,935** |
| AI Negative Reviews | **889** |
| AI Negative Share | **8.89%** |
| Hidden Negative Reviews | **377** |
| Hidden Negative Share | **42.41%** |

### 🔎 Hidden Dissatisfaction

A particularly important finding is that **377 reviews were classified as hidden negative experiences**.

These are reviews where users gave **4–5 stars**, but the written review contained negative sentiment.

This demonstrates why relying exclusively on star ratings can underestimate customer frustration.
